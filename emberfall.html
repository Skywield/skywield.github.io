Sky-island JRPG · Out now · v1.0.4

Emberfall

Explore floating isles, fight with a full party, and take the helm in airship combat. Version 1.0.4 is free on itch.io, with Steam coming after Christmas.

Play free on itch.io Play the Godot test build

Emberfall is a story-rich JRPG set among sky islands. You travel by airship, land on isles such as Reedhaven and Duskhollow, and take on bosses, duels, and ship-to-ship fights.

Version 1.0.4 is out now, free on itch.io. You can play in your browser, or download a Windows or Linux build. A Steam page will come after Christmas. A public Godot test build remains available if you want to try a work-in-progress page.
