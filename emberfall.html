Sky-island JRPG

Emberfall

Explore floating isles, fight with a full party, and take the helm in airship combat.

Play in browser Play the Godot test build

Emberfall is a story-rich JRPG set among sky islands. You travel by airship, land on isles such as Reedhaven and Duskhollow, and take on bosses, duels, and ship-to-ship fights.

The game is being rebuilt in Godot. Chapters 1–6, the sky map, and airship combat are playable in the public test build. The original web version is still up as well.
